About New-Lines

Continuing the work of Lines Magazine (2002–2004), this website continues to provide a space for dissent, to examine the narrative given to us, and to explore alternatives to socio-economic-ecological approaches. Working with numerous similar sites, we hope to help strengthen solidarity among diverse progressive voices.

Our archive holds the issues of lines magazine from 2002 to 2004, rebuilt from the original files so they can be read again.

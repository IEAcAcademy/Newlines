About New-Lines

Continuing the work of Lines Magazine (2002–2004), this website provides a space for dissent, to examine the narrative given to us, and to explore alternatives to socio-economic-ecological approaches. To this end, working with numerous similar sites, we aim to help strengthen solidarity among diverse progressive voices - Nanthi Nanthikesan

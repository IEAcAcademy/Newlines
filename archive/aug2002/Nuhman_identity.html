Identity of a Man

M. A. Nuhman

God appeared in my dream,

Opened the doors of

Heaven and Hell

‘Who are you?’ God asked me

‘I am a man’ I said

‘What’s your name?’ he asked again

‘Man’ I replied

He inquired after my ethnicity

I said ‘humanity’

‘What’s your religion?’

God asked me finally

‘Humanism’ I replied

God said with a smile

‘Yes, you can enter Heaven now’

Alas!

When my dream was over

I found myself

In Hell

-1988
